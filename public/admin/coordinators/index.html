Coordinators

Conference coordinators sign in at myplatereport.com/coord with a code sent to their email. They only see umpires tagged with their conferences, including all of those umpires' games and trends. Your own access stays on the Cloudflare sign-in.

Weekly reports go out Wednesdays at 9–10 am Pacific to you (every conference) and to each coordinator with the weekly email turned on, whenever their staff has new games from the past week.
